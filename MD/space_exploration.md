# Space Exploration & Engineering

## Enzmann Starships
Conceptual designs for interstellar spacecraft, particularly the iconic Enzmann Starship, which proposed a massive interstellar vehicle powered by nuclear fusion and carrying hundreds of people.

## Planetology & Astronomy
Includes writings and illustrations on planetary formation, surface conditions, and cosmological events that shaped the solar system.

## Cosmology
Dr. Enzmann’s interpretation of the structure of the universe, often blending scientific observation with theoretical metaphysics.
